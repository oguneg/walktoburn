Why go slow and steep?

It's faster than it looks

Adding incline raises your calorie burn rate more than adding speed does. A slow 2 mph walk at 12% incline often burns 100 calories quicker than a brisk 4 mph walk on the flat.

It's easier on your joints

Lower speed means less impact per step. Incline shifts the work to your muscles instead of your joints — you can keep this up day after day.

It's actually sustainable

You can hold a slow incline pace for a long time without redlining. Consistency beats intensity — a burn you repeat beats a burn you dread.
